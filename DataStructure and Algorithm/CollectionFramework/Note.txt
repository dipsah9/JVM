Lets learn the java Collection Framework in a playful way. 

Collection as a Box of Toy: 

Just imagine we have a box of toy. 
    -List: Here we put our collection of toys and here we can keep the same toy twice. (example, we have alredy a 
    teddybear, we can add another teddybear , that should not be  a problem)

    -Set: Here we can put each unique toy, here duplicates are not allowed. so if have teddy bear already in the box, 
    then the other teddybear is not allowed. And the good things is we know where our toy is located. 

    -Map:  Here we can organize our toy according to their name.  For example, teddy bear is labelled with the key 
    teddy, here also we can not have the duplicates.

    -Queue: Here oour toys are standing in the line, and the toy which goes in last, will come last, its works on the principle 
    first in first out. (FIFO)
